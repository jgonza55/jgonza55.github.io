Research

My research interests lie in the field of computer algebra. To be precise, I have two main focuses: regular chains and multivariate formal Puiseux series.

Regular chains

Regular chains are triangular sets of polynomials used to solve systems of polynomial equations. My research mainly focuses on one-dimensional regular chains, that is, regular chains where the number of equations is one less than the number of variables. In particular, I am interested in computing the intersection between a one-dimensional structure and a polynomial by means of evaluation and interpolation, under certain hypotheses.

Multivariate Puiseux series

Multivariate Puiseux series are a generalization of multivariate power series in which we allow rational exponents in our variables. I am currently encoding multivariate formal Puiseux series as an object inside Maple by means of lazy evaluation. I am also working on the Puiseux theorem, which allows us to factorize univariate polynomials with Puiseux series coefficients.
